How to play

Ready in under a minute

  1. Install

    Download FunAdda from Google Play. No account needed.

  2. Pick a game

    Choose one of the five games on the home screen.

  3. Set it up

    Add player nicknames, then choose a mode or category (and a timer or player count where the game uses one).

  4. Play

    Follow the on-screen prompts and pass the phone. Each game has a built-in instruction guide.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is FunAdda free?

Yes. FunAdda is free to download and play. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need internet?

No. All games work fully offline.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no accounts or logins. You only type nicknames for the players.

How many players can play?

Truth or Dare, Would You Rather, and Never Have I Ever support 2 to 6 players. Truth or Bluff supports 3 to 10. Charades is played by a group taking turns with the phone.

Is my data collected?

No. Player names and sound settings are stored only on your device. FunAdda has no ads, analytics, or tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Is it suitable for kids?

This game contains mature themes and is strictly reserved for players aged 18 and over.

What if a dare feels unsafe or uncomfortable?

Skip it. Every player can pass on any truth or dare at any time. Never do anything dangerous, illegal, or that makes anyone uncomfortable. Players are responsible for their own actions, as explained in the Terms & Conditions.

Which Android versions are supported?

FunAdda runs on Android 7.0 (Nougat) and newer, on phones and tablets.

How do I delete my saved player names?

Tap the delete icon beside a name in the player list, or clear the app’s data in your Android settings.
