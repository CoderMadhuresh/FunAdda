2. Information We Do Not Collect

FunAdda is designed to work without collecting personal data. We do not collect, request, store on our servers, or receive any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any account details (the App has no accounts or sign-in)
  • Precise or approximate location
  • IP address, advertising ID, device identifiers, or device fingerprints
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, videos, files, or audio recordings
  • Camera or microphone data (the App does not use them)
  • Payment or financial information (the App has no purchases)
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural profiles
  • Chat messages or voice communication between players (the App has no chat)

Because the App does not send data to us, we have no copy of your information and cannot look at it, sell it, or share it.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

To make the games convenient, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device using Android’s private app storage (known as “SharedPreferences”). This data never leaves your device through the App, and other apps cannot normally access it.

Data What it is Why it is stored Where it goes
Player names Nicknames you type when setting up Truth or Dare, Would You Rather, or Never Have I Ever (up to 10 characters each) So you do not have to re-enter your group’s names every time Stays on your device. Delete a name with the delete icon, or clear the App’s data.
Audio settings Whether background music and sound effects are turned on or off To remember your preference Stays on your device
Temporary game state Selected game mode, category, timer, turn order, scores, and any word you type during a round (for example in Truth or Bluff) To run the current game Held in memory only and discarded when the game or App closes. Not saved to storage.

A tip: player names are only labels. Please use nicknames, and avoid entering full names, phone numbers, or other sensitive information into name fields.

4. Multiplayer and Gameplay

FunAdda’s multiplayer is local, same-device play: friends take turns using one phone or tablet. The App does not include online matchmaking, lobbies, accounts, friend lists, or chat, and it does not send gameplay data over the internet.

If we ever add online features, we will update this policy before they are released and tell you what data they use (for example, any network identifiers) and why.

5. Device Sensors, Permissions and Internet Access

Motion sensor (accelerometer). In Charades, the App reads your device’s motion sensor to detect when you lift and tilt the phone, so a round can start hands-free. These readings are processed instantly on your device to trigger the game action. They are not recorded, stored, or transmitted. This sensor does not require a permission prompt on Android.

Internet permission. Playing the games does not require an internet connection. The App requests internet access only so that a small number of optional features work: opening the “Share App” sheet, opening the App’s Google Play listing from “Rate Us”, opening the feedback form described below, and opening this Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions from within the App. These features only use the internet at the moment you tap them; the App does not otherwise send data over the internet in the background.

Other permissions. The App does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or phone.

6. Third-Party Services and SDKs

FunAdda does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-login, attribution, or payment SDKs. The App is built with standard open-source Android and Jetpack libraries, which run locally on your device and do not send data to us.

A few things you may choose to do involve other services, which have their own privacy practices:

  • Google Play. You download and update the App through Google Play. Google handles that relationship under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms. We do not receive your personal details from Google.
  • Android backup. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the App’s saved data (such as player names and audio settings) in your device backup to your Google Account. This is handled by Google and your device settings, not by us. You can turn it off in your device’s system settings.
  • Share App. Opens your device’s share sheet so you can send a link to the App. What you share, and with whom, is your choice.
  • Rate Us. Opens the App’s page on Google Play.
  • Feedback form. Opens a Google Form in your browser, hosted by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See “Feedback (Google Forms)” below.
  • Privacy Policy and Terms links. Open this website in your browser.

8. Data Retention and Deleting Your Data

Because everything the App saves is on your device, you control it:

  • Remove a player name: tap the delete icon next to the name in the player list.
  • Delete everything the App has saved: on Android go to Settings → Apps → FunAdda → Storage → Clear data (wording varies by device), or uninstall the App.
  • Backups: if you use Android backup, remove the App’s backup from your Google Account settings.

The App has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete and no server-side gameplay data for us to erase. For any feedback you sent us, see “Feedback and Contacting Us” above.

9. Data Security

The App keeps its data in Android’s private app storage and does not transmit it, which greatly limits exposure. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and the safety of the data on your device also depends on your device’s own protections (screen lock, OS updates, and so on).
